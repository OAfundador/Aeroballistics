# Sources

- `jp2/` — high-resolution scan of the report (DTIC AD0915628, Distribution A): 96 JP2 pages of ~2600×3400 px, `DTIC_AD0915628_0000.jp2` to `_0095.jp2`. File index = printed page + 3. Extracted from `DTIC_AD0915628_jp2.zip`.
- `cache/` — pages already rotated and autocontrasted, generated by `scripts/reading/crop.py`. Can be deleted.

- `BRL620_Hitchcock.pdf` — Hitchcock, *Aerodynamic Data for Spinning Projectiles*, BRL Report 620 (AD-800 469), used in `scripts/free_flight/hitchcock/` and in the inertia formulas of `aeroballistics.mass` (p. 9). Pages extracted with `scripts/reading/pdf_page.py`.
- `MR1833_Piddington.pdf` — BRL MR 1833 (AD815788), 7.62 NATO family, used in `scripts/free_flight/mr1833/`.

- Free-flight benchmarks (archive.org, DTIC, all cleared for public release), transcribed in `data/free_flight/`:
  - `DTIC_AD0454925.pdf` — Karpov et al., 155 mm M101 (BRL MR 1582, 1964)
  - `DTIC_ADA235620.pdf` — Whyte, 155 mm M483A1 (BRL-CR-659, 1991)
  - `DTIC_ADA219106.pdf` — McCoy, .50 M33/M8/M20 (BRL-MR-3810, 1990)
  - `DTIC_ADA162133.pdf` — McCoy, 5.56 mm NATO (BRL-MR-3476, Oct. 1985)
  - `DTIC_ADA205633.pdf` — McCoy, 7.62 mm match: M118, 190 gr and 168 gr Sierra (BRL-MR-3733, Dec. 1988)
  - `DTIC_ADA121258.pdf` — McCoy, 30 mm XM788E1 and XM789 (ARBRL-TR-03432, Oct. 1982)
  - `DTIC_ADA086096.pdf` — McCoy, 30 mm XM788 (ARBRL-MR-03019, May 1980) *
  - `DTIC_AD0086528.pdf` — Karpov, Skeggs and Hull, 175 mm T203 and square base, 90 mm models (BRL MR 956, Dec. 1955) *
  - `DTIC_AD0857512.pdf` — Brandon, 152 mm XM617 (BRL MR 1998, Jul. 1969) *
  - Downloaded and **not used**:
    - `DTIC_ADA229713.pdf` — McCoy, .22 LR match (BRL-MR-3877, 1990): stepped base ("heel") and rounded nose, outside what the SPIN-73 geometry describes.
    - `DTIC_AD0078604.pdf` — Roecker, 105 mm M1 (BRL MR 929, 1955): the report only gives the length (≈4.7 cal) and the boattail (0.5 cal, 9°); the ogive and its radius are not dimensioned, and p. 47 of SPIN-73, which would carry the M1 card, repeats p. 44 in the scan.
    - `DTIC_AD0729238.pdf` — Regan and Schermerhorn, 20 mm Navy GP (NOLTR 71-95, 1971): plots only, no round-by-round table.

  \* DTIC "MRC" PDFs: the text sits in a JBIG2 mask, which Pillow cannot read and neither can the Windows PDF reader. They were read with `scripts/reading/pdf_pages.py`, which uses the pure-Python JBIG2 decoder in `scripts/reading/jbig2.py` (any PDF reader based on PDFium or pdf.js shows the same pages).

None of these files is versioned (see `.gitignore`): they are large and available from DTIC.
